About the Trust

Swami Vivekanand Students Welfare Charitable Trust was established on June 10, 1996 with the aim of fostering the holistic development of underprivileged girl students.

What Makes Us Unique

Beyond Financial Support

Unlike conventional institutions, the Trust provides continuous guidance, mentorship, and personal support to every student.

Need-Based Scholarships

Scholarships are awarded purely on the basis of need, ensuring dignity and equal opportunity for every girl.

Holistic Development

With consistent guidance and value-based nurturing, students grow academically and personally, often exceeding expectations.

Our Objectives

Fostering Curiosity & Thinking

Encourage original thinking, creativity, and inquisitiveness so that students are prepared to face future challenges with confidence.

Strengthening Academic Foundation

Support students in their educational journey through guidance, study techniques, problem-solving, and access to essential learning resources.

Building Confidence & Self-Reliance

Instill self-esteem, confidence, and independence while nurturing positive, rational, and disciplined thinking.

Promoting Balanced & Peaceful Living

Teach the art of living with happiness and balance, enabling students to remain calm and resilient even in challenging situations.

Holistic Personality Development

Provide guidance for overall personality development, including emotional strength, mental well-being, and freedom from stress.

Creating Responsible Citizens

Encourage awareness of rights and duties, respect for nature, and inspire students towards self-employment, entrepreneurship, and social responsibility.

Our Guiding Principles

Equal Opportunity for All

No girl should be deprived of education due to financial constraints. Support is provided purely on the basis of need, irrespective of caste, religion, or background.

Dignity & Self-Respect

Every student is nurtured with care, ensuring her dignity, grace, and self-respect are always upheld.

Beyond Financial Support

The Trust goes beyond financial aid by providing continuous guidance and mentorship for holistic personality development.

Value-Based Living

Students are encouraged to cultivate compassion, tolerance, positivity, and gratitude, enabling them to grow with purpose and inner strength.

Transparency & Commitment

Administrative expenses are kept minimal and are borne by Trust members, ensuring maximum support reaches those in need.

Our Philosophy & Approach

Rooted in Values & Dignity

Shri Nandkishore Sharda believed that education is a natural right of every individual, especially the girl child. The Trust ensures that every student is treated with dignity and self-respect, where support is not seen as charity, but as rightful empowerment.

Special emphasis is placed on nurturing curiosity, discipline, and dedication from an early age. Alongside academic learning, strong cultural values and ethics are instilled to ensure true holistic development.

Building Confident & Capable Individuals

Through Personality Development and Sanskar Classes, students are guided to define their goals, understand them, and take meaningful action with clarity and confidence.

This approach strengthens decision-making ability, self-awareness, and emotional balance — enabling students to grow into self-reliant individuals who contribute positively to family, society, and the nation.
