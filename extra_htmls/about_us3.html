The Reality We Witnessed

Two decades ago across Western Rajasthan, higher education remained a distant, impossible milestone for girl students facing extreme systemic financial hurdles, while local setups lacked baseline support networks.

Stepping into the Cause

Faced with these multi-layered barriers, Shri Nandkishore Ji Sharda (fondly addressed as “Bhaiya”) chose to dedicate his life directly to shielding and validating these marginalized young women.

He realized that academic access is only half the battle. To uplift a family, education must be securely anchored by ethical responsibility, personal core integrity, and practical modern execution structures.

01

Academic Access

Providing critical financial stability, material packages, and baseline security support so learning remains unhindered.

02

Moral Roots (Sanskar)

Cultivating humility, ethics, and values. Bhaiya Ji routinely advised that skill without moral standing results in narrow self-arrogance.

03

Self-Reliance

Weaving tactile vocational skills alongside technological digital safety to build self-sustaining independent careers.
