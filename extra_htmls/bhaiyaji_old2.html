Shri Nandkishore Sharda

"When Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari blessed me with her divine vision, I ran and prostrated at her feet."


– these words are written in the handwritten diary of an 18 year-old youth, which he started writing at the tender age of 12. After reading this, curiosity must have arisen in your mind that in this materialistic era, who can be such a young man who has blessed with the divine vision of Divine Mother?
The name of this highly revered young man is Nandkishore Sharda.

The Seed of Spiritual Awakening

How His Divine Journey Began

Shri Nandkishore Sharda, lovingly called 'Bhaiya ji', was born on July 16, 1944, in Jodhpur, in the spiritually rich family of Smt. Tulsi Devi and Shri Vijaykishan Ji Sharda. A brilliant science student with exceptional intellect and a magnetic personality, he was admired by all.

In 1959, a life-changing moment transformed a 15-year-old boy forever.

Shri Nandkishore Sharda, young and full of innocence, was struck by the sudden demise of his father. The loss was not just personal — it opened his eyes to the profound truth of life’s impermanence.
For the first time, he realized that this physical body, so cherished and cared for, is fragile and mortal.

From that moment onward, his heart turned away from the fleeting attractions of the material world. A burning quest arose within him—an unshakable determination to seek the cosmic power that breathes life into the body.
What is that eternal force?
How does it govern us?
What is the true purpose of human existence?
How should one walk this path of life?

Thus began his extraordinary spiritual journey—one that would not only define his own destiny but also illuminate the path for countless others.

The Genesis of His Spiritual Journey

The life of Shri Nandkishore Sharda is a testament to the power of sincerity, purity of thought and unwavering devotion. His journey was not inherited, but discovered—step by step—through an honest quest for truth that ultimately led him to the Divine Mother’s grace.

Seeking Truth Beyond Blind Faith

Shri Nandkishore Sharda sought truth through reason, not blind faith. He visited many sadhus, tantriks, saints and mahatmas on his quest for deeper spiritual understanding but found their answers unscientific and dogmatic.

He believed that truth is eternal, yet the path to realize it must evolve with time. Guided by intellect and sincerity, he pursued spiritual truth beyond tradition, ritual and superstition.

The Call of the Divine Mother

While walking through the valleys near the Jodhpur fort, he noticed a temple of Maa Tripurasundari perched atop a hillock (Tekri). Drawn by a sacred inner pull, he climbed the hill and felt deep peace and tranquility inside the temple.

This moment marked the beginning of his complete surrender and lifelong devotion to the Divine Mother.

Blessed with Divine Vision

The Divine Mother, moved by his innocence and sincerity, blessed him with her divine vision and bestowed spiritual powers upon him. He used these gifts not for himself, but entirely for the welfare of humanity.

The Radiance of Divine Grace

Illuminating the Path of Devotees, Now and Always

Bhaiya ji undertook the most intense and sacred sadhna, yet out of his infinite compassion, he revealed to us a simple and gentle path to reach the Divine Mother.

He taught that when one offers sadhna with unwavering faith, pure devotion, heartfelt love, and sincere dedication, the Divine Mother’s grace naturally descends—and through that grace, Bhaiya ji lovingly unites the devotee with Her. His exalted thoughts, his virtuous deeds, his divine wisdom, and the depth of his spiritual experiences continue to light the way for innumerable seekers.

For thousands, he is not only a guide but a living inspiration—leading them toward progress both in the material world and on the spiritual path.

All this radiance, all this transformation, has been made possible solely through the boundless blessings and divine compassion of the Mother upon Her beloved child, Bhaiya ji.

Revelations of the Divine

The Ambrosial Knowledge and the unique Sādhanā technique for all ages

The sacred and ambrosial knowledge that Bhaiya ji received through his highest-order sadhna under the loving guidance of Divine Mother Maa Tripursundari has been beautifully shared in his divine book, “Wonderful World After Death”.

Starting with science and then going beyond its realm, he has explained the origin and formation of the universe, the divine powers and their abodes, the importance of divine powers, the ultimate goal of human life, etc. in this book. Reading this book builds unshakeable trust and faith in the Almighty and divinity.

Through his unwavering dedication, sincerity, truthfulness, renunciation, and single-minded pursuit of the Divine, Shri Nandkishore ji Sharda mastered the four most profound and challenging paths of sadhana— Bhakti Yoga, Gyan Yoga, Nishkam Karma Yoga, and Yoga Sadhana.

Realizing that these disciplines are difficult for the common person to follow in today’s fast-paced era, he compassionately simplified and integrated their essence into a single, practical, and deeply transformative technique: “Buddhi-Vivek Yoga Sadhana”. This contemporary method empowers individuals to progress both materially and spiritually, ultimately leading to true happiness, inner peace, and divine bliss.

Bhaiya ji’s contribution to humanity is extraordinary and unparalleled in this age. Responding to the needs of the present era, he offered a new ideology, a new technique, and new divine knowledge across physical, social, and spiritual domains—guiding the formation of a dedicated group committed to restoring true human values.

A Life Devoted to Humanity

From Divine Grace to Selfless Social Service

After years of deep sadhna, Shri Sharda and Basantiji were divinely inspired in 1996 to dedicate their lives to social upliftment. Recognizing the urgent need for girls’ education—especially in western Rajasthan, where poverty and neglect dimmed their future—they resolved to uplift them with both knowledge and values.

With Maa Tripursundari’s grace, they founded the Swami Vivekanand Students Welfare Charitable Trust, laying the true foundation of women empowerment.

They believed that education must go beyond academics and be rooted in sanskar and spirituality, nurturing a girl’s life with compassion, sincerity, inner strength, and divine values, so she grows into a confident, enlightened, and empowered individual.

Their mission focused on the most underprivileged girls—those ignored by society and overlooked even at home. With values, love, and spiritual grounding, the Trust began transforming young lives and preparing a generation committed to restoring human qualities in society.

For its unwavering dedication to girls’ education and women’s empowerment, the Rajasthan government honored the Trust with the Bhamashah Award for five consecutive years (1999–2003).

Bhaiyaji, however, never saw these awards as personal accolades. He viewed them as recognition of the resilient students who, despite facing immense challenges, continue to strive for a brighter future. The Trust’s remarkable success, he believed, belonged entirely to these determined students.

From Divine Journey to Eternal Remembrance

On January 24, 2003, auspicious occasion of Vivekananda Jayanti, he left this mortal world for the divine abode. While living in the world, he remained untouched by all worldly vices and dedicated himself incessantly towards selfless work and presented a perfect example of ‘Sthit Pragya’ life- the perfect sage.

The “Manidweep Ka Adhyatma Purush- Shri Nandkishore Sharda” , a memoir on his personality and accomplishments, comprises articles of more than 350 eminent persons from all walks of life, expressing their intimate thoughts and paying their homage and tribute to him.

The personality, works and accomplishments of Bhaiya ji are described in detail in the book “Maa Tripurasundari Ke Laadle Chakradhari Yuga Pravartak Adhyatmayogi Shri Nandkishore Sharda”. This book also includes a few pages of his handwritten diary which deeply reflects his devotion towards the Divine Mother, his philosophical thoughts, and the divine knowledge and experiences gained through the Divine Mother.

To honor Bhaiyaji Shri Nandkishore Sharda’s profound work in knowledge, spiritual practice, and women’s education, and to ensure that his devoted actions continue to inspire generations, the local administration has named roads after him in Jodhpur, Balotra, and Jasol as an enduring remembrance.
