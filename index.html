Our Vision & Mission

Empowering girls through education and holistic development, rooted in compassion, values, and self-reliance. We ensure no girl is denied education because of financial hardship, caste, community, or religion — nurturing confident, self-reliant changemakers who lead with strength and purpose.

The Spirit of Our Trust

The principles that guide our actions and define our commitment to every girl we support.

Need-Based, Not Merit-Based

Support is extended purely on genuine need — regardless of marks, religion, caste, or background.

A Right, Not a Favour

We treat this as every girl's rightful share, not charity — so she can accept help with dignity and pride.

Every Contribution, Direct Impact

Administrative costs are borne voluntarily by trust members, so your donation goes entirely to education.

Transparency in Every Step

Whether through school recommendations or direct outreach to college students, we ensure every girl receives her rightful share — with dignity.

The Pillars of our mission

The Trust's work is inspired by the teachings of these spiritual leaders, whose guidance shaped its values of dignity, service, and self-reliance — while all donations are directed solely toward education.

Our Inspiration

Shri Nandkishore Sharda

A profound spiritual seeker and visionary who, through selfless devotion and disciplined practice, founded the Trust in 1996 to give underprivileged girls in western Rajasthan both education and values.

Guiding Force
