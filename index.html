Our Vision & Mission

Empowering girls through education and holistic development, rooted in compassion, values, and self-reliance. Nurturing confident individuals with a positive outlook to lead meaningful lives. Shaping future changemakers who contribute with strength and purpose to society.

We bring this vision to life by ensuring that every girl has access to education while nurturing confidence, values, and self-reliance — empowering her to lead a dignified and purposeful life. This commitment is carried forward through two key focus areas:

Education Without Barriers

We ensure that no girl is denied education due to financial constraints, regardless of caste, community, or religion—creating equal opportunity for every child to learn, grow, and thrive.

Confidence & Character

Through regular engagement with schools, we nurture self-confidence, dignity, and inner strength among girls—guiding them to value education, embrace strong human values, and move toward a self-reliant and purposeful future.

Beyond education, we inspire a way of life rooted in discipline, purpose, and compassion.

The Spirit of Our Trust

The principles that guide our actions and define our commitment to every girl we support

Need-Based, Not Merit-Based

Financial assistance is extended purely on the basis of genuine need, without regard to academic performance, religion, caste, or background — giving every girl a fair and equal chance at education.

A Right, Not a Favour

We believe this support is not an act of charity, but every girl’s rightful share in the Divine plan. This belief preserves self-respect, nurtures inner strength, and teaches her to accept help with grace, gratitude, and pride.

Every Contribution, Direct Impact

Every donation received is dedicated entirely to scholarship distribution. Administrative expenses are minimal and covered voluntarily, ensuring that your contribution directly benefits deserving students.

The Pillars of our mission

The Trust's work is inspired by the teachings of these spiritual leaders, whose guidance shaped its values of dignity, service, and self-reliance — while all donations are directed solely toward education.

Our Inspiration

Shri Nandkishore Sharda

He was a profound spiritual seeker and visionary who, through selfless devotion, complete surrender, and disciplined spiritual practice, attained the grace and direct realization of Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari. Guided by Her divine wisdom, he shared teachings relevant to the modern age and inspired countless individuals to recognize spirituality as a living reality capable of transforming lives.

For the holistic development of humanity and the realization of life’s true purpose, he introduced “Buddhi Vivek Yog Sadhna” — a simple, universal, and practical spiritual discipline. Through this path, individuals are guided towards a harmonious balance between material responsibilities and spiritual growth, enabling them to lead lives rooted in peace, clarity, joy, and inner fulfillment.

By beautifully integrating wisdom, devotion, and selfless service in his own life, he established a timeless ideal that inspired and transformed thousands of lives, paving the way for a new era of spiritual awakening and purposeful living.

Guiding Force

Kumari Basanti Manihar

She chose the spiritual path in 1964. Under the guidance and spiritual empowerment of her mentor, Shri Nandkishore Ji Sharda, she attained the grace of Jagatjanani Maa through childlike devotion, unwavering dedication, and complete surrender. After Bhaiyaji assumed his chaitanya (divine conscious) form, she carried forward his divine knowledge and spiritual methodology, skillfully advancing all the noble initiatives he had begun.

By resolving the spiritual and practical questions of thousands of seekers, she brought about a positive transformation in their outlook on life and taught them the art of living with joy, peace, and inner happiness. Alongside this, she sowed the seeds of education, values, and spirituality in the lives of thousands of students— especially girls—so that they might grow into responsible and capable citizens. Bhaiyaji and Maa Basanti Ji were true pioneers of women’s empowerment.

Continuing the Legacy

Kumari Madhubala Advani

She embraced the spiritual path in 1970 under the guidance of Maa Basanti and Bhaiyaji. Through rigorous spiritual disciplines imparted by them, she was blessed with the grace of numerous divine powers. For the past 55 years, she has remained steadfastly dedicated to spiritual practice.

After Maa Basanti left for heavenly abode in year 2021, Madhu Maa has been seamlessly and devotedly carrying forward all the responsibilities entrusted by Mother and Bhaiyaji. These include the administration of all Trust activities, publication and distribution of spiritual literature, nurturing education, values, and spirituality among students, guiding seekers associated with “Manidweep” through spiritual discourses, distributing food grains to the needy, and running free yoga classes and a tailoring center, among other service initiatives.
