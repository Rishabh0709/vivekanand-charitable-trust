Swami Vivekanand Students’ Welfare Charitable Trust

Blending education with sanskar for true women empowerment

Every girl deserves the light of education, none should be deprived of it because of poverty.

Swami Vivekanand Students’ Welfare Charitable Trust was founded on 10th June 1996 by Kumari Basanti Manihar and Shri Nandkishore Sharda with the divine grace and blessings of divine mother Maa Tripursundari. Kumari Madhubala Advani joined the trust at its inception as a selfless dedicated grassroots worker under Shri Sharda’s leadership.

At that time, girl's education was one of the most pressing needs, especially in Western Rajasthan, where girls were often seen as a burden due to poverty and illiteracy

To change this reality, this trust laid the true foundation of women empowerment by extending education and support to underprivileged girls. These girls, once surrounded by poverty, illiteracy, and despair — unseen by society and unheard by their families — found a new path through the Trust’s compassionate care and guidance.

Through education and moral nurturing, they have discovered strength, self-worth, and a new light of hope

Our Philosophy

Our Vision

The Spirit of Our Trust

Empowering Education, Enriching Lives

No girl should ever be deprived of education for want of money. Regardless of marks, religion, or background, every deserving child is given a chance to learn and dream. Financial help is extended purely on the basis of need, ensuring every girl continues her journey with dignity.

📚 Education for Every Girl

The Trust transforms fear into faith and uncertainty into confidence — empowering girls to live with courage, dignity, and purpose.

🕊️ From Fear to Faith

Every donation received is dedicated entirely to scholarship distribution. Administrative expenses are minimal and covered voluntarily, ensuring that your contribution directly benefits deserving students.

💸🎓 Every Rupee for Education

We believe this support is not an act of charity, but every girl’s rightful share in the Divine plan. This belief preserves self-respect, nurtures inner strength, and teaches her to accept help with grace, gratitude, and pride.

💫 Not Charity, But Her Rightful Share

Through scholarships and Sanskar classes, we nurture compassion, gratitude, and moral strength — raising young women who are not only educated but enlightened, ready to illuminate the world with their goodness. This nurturing bond creates a deep sense of belonging — the girls know the Trust stands with them, always

🌱 Nurturing Hearts and Minds

To nurture core values, girls are inspired to embrace 3 sacred resolutions—simple yet transformative steps toward inner and outer growth:
🙏Faith: Offer daily prayers with selfless devotion and gratitude for atleast 10 mins.
🎯Focus: Pursue academic goals with dedication and discipline.
❤️Fulfillment: Give back by supporting other sisters, fostering compassion and belonging.
