Swami Vivekanand Students’ Welfare Charitable Trust A Ray Of Hope...

Swami Vivekanand Students’ Welfare Charitable Trust was founded on 10th June 1996 by Kumari Basanti Manihar and Shri Nandkishore Sharda with the divine grace of Jagatjanani Maa Tripursundari. Kumari Madhubala Advani joined the trust at its inception as a selfless grassroots worker under Shri Sharda’s leadership.

Girl's education was one of the most pressing needs, especially in Western Rajasthan, where girls were often seen as a burden due to poverty and illiteracy. To change this reality, this trust laid the foundation of women empowerment by extending education and support to underprivileged girls. These girls, once surrounded by poverty and despair, found a new path through the Trust’s care and guidance.

Through education and moral nurturing, they have discovered strength, self-worth, and a new light of hope.

Our Vision

To create a Trust that would support the education of girls through financial assistance while nurturing their complete, holistic development. We envision empowering young women to cultivate a broad, farsighted, and positive outlook on life, rooted in enduring human values such as generosity, tolerance, compassion, and love. By fostering self-reliance, self-confidence, and steadfast commitment to their aspirations, the Trust seeks to enable girls to build a distinct and dignified identity, face future challenges with strength and clarity, lead joyful and purposeful lives, and ultimately emerge as responsible, enlightened, and invaluable contributors to the nation — guided by a harmonious balance between individual rights and responsibilities.

Our Mission

Empowering girls through education, values, and self-reliance

Education Without Discrimination

We ensure that no girl studying in any government or community-run school adopted by us is deprived of education due to financial constraints, irrespective of her caste, community, or religion.

Holistic Development & Self-Belief

Through regular school visits and inspiring interactions, we nurture self-respect, confidence, dignity, and core human values — guiding girls toward a self-reliant and purposeful life.

Cycle of Gratitude & Service

To nuture core values, we inspire girls to pray daily, pursue education with determination, and once independent, support other needy sisters—continuing this sacred Gyan Yagna of service and belonging.

The Pillars of Our Mission

Yugpravartak Shri Nandkishore Sharda

He was an exceptional thinker and spiritual practitioner who, through selfless love, complete surrender, and rigorous spiritual discipline, attained direct realization of Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari. Accepting Her as his Guru, he received knowledge suited to the modern age and demonstrated that God is a living reality who grants direct spiritual experience.

For the holistic development of humanity and the fulfillment of life’s true purpose, he offered a simple, universal, and effective practice of "Buddhi Vivek Yog Sadhna". Through this path, balance is established between material and spiritual life, enabling individuals to live stress-free, joyful, peaceful, and meaningful lives.

By harmoniously integrating knowledge, devotion, and selfless action in his own life, he set a powerful ideal that inspired and transformed the lives of thousands, ushering in the auspicious beginning of a new era.

Maa Basanti Manihar

She chose the spiritual path in 1964. Under the guidance and spiritual empowerment of her mentor, Shri Nandkishor Ji Sharda, she attained the grace of Jagatjanani Maa through childlike devotion, unwavering dedication, and complete surrender. After Bhaiyaji assumed his chaitanya (divine conscious) form, she carried forward his divine knowledge and spiritual methodology, skillfully advancing all the noble initiatives he had begun.

By resolving the spiritual and practical questions of thousands of seekers, she brought about a positive transformation in their outlook on life and taught them the art of living with joy, peace, and inner happiness. Alongside this, she sowed the seeds of education, values, and spirituality in the lives of thousands of students—especially girls—so that they might grow into responsible and capable citizens. Bhaiyaji and Mother Basanti Ji were true pioneers of women’s empowerment.

Madhu Advani (Madhu Maa)

She embraced the spiritual path in 1970 under the guidance of Maa Basanti and Bhaiyaji. Through rigorous spiritual disciplines imparted by them, she was blessed with the grace of numerous divine powers. For the past 55 years, she has remained steadfastly dedicated to spiritual practice.

After Maa Basanti left for heavenly abode in year 2021, Madhu Maa has been seamlessly and devotedly carrying forward all the responsibilities entrusted by Mother and Bhaiyaji. These include the administration of all Trust activities, publication and distribution of spiritual literature, nurturing education, values, and spirituality among students, guiding seekers associated with “Manidweep” through spiritual discourses, distributing food grains to the needy, and running free yoga classes and a tailoring center, among other service initiatives.
