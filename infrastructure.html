Basic Infrastructure Support

Bhaiya Shri Nandkishore Sharda realized that unless basic facilities are provided along with financial assistance, the effort for education would remain incomplete. Therefore, to resolve the fundamental challenges in schools, he arranged for the construction of classrooms, reception rooms, toilets, water-huts, boundary walls, and essential furniture—helping girls rise above an environment of neglect and scarcity.

Shri Sharda personally contributed financially toward building furniture, classrooms, reception halls, water-huts, toilets, and boundary walls in many schools, and inspired fellow trust members to participate. He also mobilized support from the District Collector, MPs, MLAs, and other public leaders.

Constructed the Shri Nandkishore Sharda Reception Hall and three classrooms in Mahalaxmi Girls' School, Pratapnagar, Jodhpur.

Financial support provided for the construction of the "Kumari Basanti Manihar Commerce Block" in Mahila Mahavidyalaya, Jodhpur.

Constructed “Tulsi Water-hut” in two girls’ schools in memory of Smt. Tulsi Devi.

Arranged furniture for 16 classrooms in 7 girls’ schools.

Additionally, the Trust constructed halls, classrooms, computer labs, and facilities worth over ₹1.5 crore in schools located in Siwanchi Gate, Alpbhasha Rajmahal, Jalori Gate, Bhagat ki Kothi, Rajmahal, Mahamandir, and Sardarpura—supported by MPs, MLAs, and other contributors.
