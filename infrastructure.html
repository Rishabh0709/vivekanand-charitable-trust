Basic Infrastructure Support

A scholarship buys a textbook. But a leaking roof, a missing toilet, or no place to sit can silence a girl's education just as surely as poverty can. The Trust built what was missing.

Why Infrastructure Matters

Bhaiya Shri Nandkishore Sharda understood early that financial assistance alone could not complete the work of education. Girls arriving at schools with dilapidated buildings, broken furniture, no drinking water, and absent sanitation were fighting two battles at once — poverty at home and neglect at school.

To remove these silent barriers, he personally arranged for the construction of classrooms, reception halls, toilets, water-huts, boundary walls, and essential furniture across multiple schools — ensuring every girl had a dignified space in which to learn.

He did not stop at personal contribution. Shri Sharda mobilised support from District Collectors, Members of Parliament, MLAs, and other public leaders, turning a Trust initiative into a community responsibility.

Named Projects

Halls, classrooms, and facilities built one school at a time.

Shri Nandkishore Sharda Reception Hall Constructed the Reception Hall and three classrooms at Mahalaxmi Girls' School, Pratapnagar, Jodhpur.

Kumari Basanti Manihar Commerce Block Financial support provided for construction of the Commerce Block at Mahila Mahavidyalaya, Jodhpur.

Tulsi Water-hut Built in memory of Smt. Tulsi Devi across two girls' schools — providing clean drinking water where there was none.

Classroom Furniture Drive Arranged furniture for 16 classrooms across 7 girls' schools — so every student had a seat and a desk to call her own.
