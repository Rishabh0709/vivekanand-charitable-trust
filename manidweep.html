Manidweep

A center of devotion, faith, and divine consciousness — and the spiritual root from which this educational mission draws its values.

"Manidweep" is not just a building but a center of devotion, faith, penance, divine love, consciousness, and high moral values — where Divine Mother "Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari" herself resides. In the material world, Manidweep is the symbol of the divine abode of Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari.

It is a place brimming with sentient particles and is a seat of divine knowledge for truth seekers. Just sitting silently and meditating here fills one's heart with positive thoughts, joy, peace, and bliss.

On regularly coming here, one is filled with a feeling of compassion, sympathy, and love — arousing the feeling of universal brotherhood, or "Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam."

Swami Vivekanand Students' Welfare Charitable Trust was founded under the guidance drawn from Manidweep, but operates as an independently registered public charitable trust in its own right. Every donation made to the Trust goes 100% toward girls' education and scholarships — never toward religious activity.
