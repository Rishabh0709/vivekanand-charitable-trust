Academic Distinction

"I did not just want to pass. I wanted to excel. The Trust taught me that excellence, when rooted in gratitude and purpose, is not arrogance — it is responsibility."

— Meenu Tanwar, M.Com Gold Medalist, JNVU Jodhpur

True education goes beyond textbooks—it builds character, resilience, and an unyielding pursuit of excellence. As an M.Com student at JNVU Jodhpur, Meenu did not just navigate her academic track; she dominated it, graduating at the absolute top of her class as a Gold Medalist.

Her remarkable achievement was nurtured and sustained by the Swami Vivekanand Students Welfare Charitable Trust. By relieving her family of the mounting financial pressures of higher education, the Trust allowed Meenu to focus entirely on her studies while grounding her success in deep-rooted values of gratitude, leadership, and service to society.

Meenu Tanwar

Gold Medalist
M.Com, JNVU, Jodhpur (Raj.)
