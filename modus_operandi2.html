Modus Operandi

Shri Nandkishore Sharda's intense thinking and contemplation led to a methodology for uplifting needy girl students, addressing not just financial support but also their values, confidence, and holistic well-being.
Financial Assistance

To provide financial assistance to needy girl students, ensuring that lack of resources never interrupts their education.

Inspire Human Values & Confidence

Instilling values, boosting confidence, and strengthening character through inspirational literature.

Awaken Curiosity Towards Education

Encouraging learning for knowledge—not just exams—so curiosity and understanding become lifelong.

Instill Compassion & Spirituality

Building values like compassion, tolerance, positivity, gratitude, and daily selfless prayer for inner growth.

Promote Health, Hygiene & Well-being

Emphasizing simple habits—air, sunlight, smiling, yoga, exercise, pranayama—to ensure physical and mental wellness.

Develop Intellect & Discretion

Awakening intellect and discretion (buddhi-vivek) to help girls shape their future and claim their rightful place in society.

Foster Duties, Rights & Good Citizenship

Encouraging girls to understand both their rights and duties, becoming aware and responsible citizens.

Synergy of Stakeholders

Ensuring collaboration among parents, teachers, and students—because empowerment thrives through unity.
