Finding Stability

"When my father passed away, I nearly lost my future with him. The Trust became the steady ground our family needed — long enough for me to build my own."

— Neetu Deora, Senior Assistant, SBI

I received support from the Swami Vivekanand Students Welfare Charitable Trust for my education from 9th class (1998) through to the completion of my M.Com. in 2007. I am deeply grateful to the Trust for standing by me during one of the most challenging periods of my life—after the loss of my father.

"At that time, continuing my education seemed impossible. Our family was going through an emotionally and financially difficult phase, and had it not been for the timely support from the Trust, I would not have been able to pursue my studies."

The education I received during those crucial years not only shaped my career but also helped me earn a dignified place in society. Since 2008, I have been working at State Bank of India (SBI), and I am currently serving as a Senior Assistant.

The values and guidance imparted to me—especially by Maa Basanti Manihar, the Founder and President of the Trust—have had a lasting impact on my life. Her wisdom and support have also helped me build and maintain a happy and peaceful married life.

As a humble gesture of gratitude, I regularly contribute financially to the Trust. It is my way of giving back to the institution that helped shape my future and continues to uplift many others.

Neetu Deora

Senior Assistant SBI, Shastri Nagar Branch, Jodhpur (Raj.)
