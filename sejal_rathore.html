Global Horizons

"The Trust saw a future in me before I could see it myself. That belief carried a girl from Jodhpur to a career at a global financial institution."

— Sejal Rathore, Software Engineer, Wells Fargo

When structural ceilings threaten to limit potential, belief and timely support can change everything. For Sejal, the journey from navigating her upbringing in Jodhpur to engineering software systems for a premier global financial institution like Wells Fargo was paved by the unwavering backing of the Trust.

While financial relief cleared her path, it was the mentorship and confidence instilled by the Trust's leadership that allowed her to dream beyond local horizons. Today, she stands as a powerful example of how localized community investment can launch a girl onto the global technology stage.

Sejal Rathore

Software Engineer
Wells Fargo Ltd.
