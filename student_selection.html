Transparency & Trust

How We Select & Support Students

Every scholarship the Trust awards follows a clear, transparent process — rooted in genuine need, free from favouritism, and built to reach the girls who need it most.

How the Trust Identifies Students

Step 1 · School Girls

Recommended by Schools

Class teachers know their students best. When they identify a girl from a genuinely struggling family, they bring her name to the School Principal, who formally recommends her to the Trust.

  • Fees paid directly to the school: Scholarships, board fees, and learning materials are transferred by cheque to the school — no cash changes hands.
  • Open distribution: All resources are distributed in the presence of school authorities and community members, ensuring full accountability.
Step 2 · College Track

Proactive Follow-Through

When a girl we support completes school, Trust volunteers reach out — by postcard and phone — to check on her next steps and ensure her momentum continues into college or competitive studies.

  • Her academic goals and financial situation are reviewed carefully.
  • Career guidance and mentorship are offered alongside financial support.
  • Partial or full scholarships are arranged based on genuine need.

How Students Can Apply for Support

For School Students

Through Your School

If you are a school student whose family is struggling financially, your class teacher or Principal is your first and easiest step. You do not need to approach the Trust directly.

  • Speak honestly to your class teacher or Principal about your family's situation.
  • The school will verify your background and submit your application on your behalf.
  • Once the Trust receives the school's recommendation, support is arranged quickly.
For College Students

Come to Us Directly

If you have finished school and want to continue into a degree, vocational course, or competitive exam — but cannot afford to — come to us directly. No school referral is needed.

  • Visit our office at Manidweep, A-183 Shastri Nagar, Jodhpur.
  • Our volunteers will sit with you to understand your situation and goals.
  • Scholarship support or career mentorship will be arranged based on your need.

Do not let money be the reason education stops. Reach out to us — we will find a way forward together.
