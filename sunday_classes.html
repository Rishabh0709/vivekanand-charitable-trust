Why Sanskar Matters

Financial support opens the door. Values determine what she does once she walks through it.

Shri Nandkishore Sharda, Kumari Basanti Manihar, and Kumari Madhubala Advani held a firm conviction: education alone is incomplete without sanskar — the values that shape character. While modern education sharpens the intellect, it often leaves untouched the inner forces of ego, anger, greed, and jealousy that quietly determine how a life unfolds.

They taught that every person is blessed by God with Buddhi (intellect) and Vivek (discretion). When these two are cultivated together — through education, values, and spirituality — qualities like humility, compassion, sincerity, and generosity grow naturally.

Motivational sessions every Sunday empower girls to develop environmental awareness, a spirit of service, and reverence for nature. This blend of education and Sanskar shapes them into balanced, confident, and responsible individuals.

About the Classes

Every Sunday, we gather to nurture young minds, enrich core values, and empower resilient futures.

Classes are held every Sunday, July–December, 10:30 AM–12:30 PM, at Manidweep, A-183 Shastri Nagar, Jodhpur, and are open to students from all walks of life — no discrimination of caste, community, or religion.

Beyond the regular Sunday sessions, Trust volunteers periodically visit schools to conduct motivational talks for younger girls. Renowned speakers and dignitaries are also invited, offering students rare exposure to accomplished minds beyond their immediate world.

These sessions nurture personality, strengthen character, and build awareness of rights and responsibilities — laying the foundation for holistic development that no textbook can alone provide.

Many organisations provide financial scholarships. Very few actively monitor progress or provide personal guidance. The Trust fills this crucial gap.

Under Madhubalaji's leadership, a dedicated team of qualified professionals provides continuous academic support, mentoring, counselling, and life-skills training — turning an isolated scholarship into a sustained relationship.

Most girls initially lack awareness about time management, exam techniques, or the deeper purpose of education. Through this sustained mentorship, they receive comprehensive guidance that transforms their academic journey and expands their aspirations in ways they could not have imagined alone.

Academic & Professional Growth

Mentorship at the Trust fosters self-confidence, positive thinking, and a genuine love for learning. Girls begin studying for knowledge instead of marks — and academic performance follows naturally.

With education, values, and spirituality as their foundation, many go on to pursue Engineering, Medicine, Nursing, MBA, CA, MCA, and other professional degrees — becoming financially independent and socially contributing citizens.

The Trust also supports self-employment: skills in tailoring, computer literacy, and vocational training give girls pathways to livelihood that do not depend on a single employer or a single exam.
