Sanskar And Personality Development Classes

Nurturing Minds, Enriching Values, Empowering Futures

Importance of Sanskar Along with Educational Support

Shri Nandkishore Sharda, Kumari Basanti Manihar, and Kumari Madhubala Advani believed that education alone is incomplete without sanskar — the values that shape character. While modern education strengthens intellect, it often lacks the moral grounding needed to overcome ego, anger, greed, and jealousy.

According to them, every human being is blessed by God with Buddhi (intellect) and Vivek (discretion). When refined through education, values, and spirituality, individuals develop humility, compassion, sincerity, generosity, and a positive outlook on life.

Motivational and value-based sessions empower girls to develop environmental awareness, a spirit of service, and reverence for nature. This harmonious blend of education and sanskar helps them evolve into balanced, confident, and responsible individuals

About Classes

Sanskar and Personality Development Classes are conducted every Sunday (July–December, 10:30 AM–12:30 PM) at the Trust headquarters, “Manidweep,” A-183, Shastri Nagar, Jodhpur (Raj.), welcoming students from all walks of life.

From time to time, volunteers also conduct motivational sessions in schools to inspire and guide young girls. Renowned speakers and dignitaries are invited to share their knowledge and experience, offering valuable exposure and learning. These sessions nurture personality, strengthen character, and build awareness of rights and responsibilities—laying the foundation for holistic development.

While many organizations provide financial scholarships, very few actively monitor progress or provide personal guidance. The Trust fills this crucial gap. Under Madhubalaji’s leadership, a dedicated team of qualified professionals provides continuous academic support, mentoring, counselling, and life-skills training. These sessions help the girls appreciate the true value of education and gradually increase their interest in learning.

With regular motivation, personal and career guidance, and emotional support, the girls begin learning for knowledge rather than just marks, leading to a clear and steady improvement in their academic performance.

To preserve the girls’ self-esteem, they are always reminded that the scholarship is not charity — but their rightful due. This gentle yet powerful message ensured they never felt pity or dependence. Instead, they accepted support with dignity, confidence, and pride.

Most girls initially lack awareness about time management, exam techniques, or the larger purpose of education. Through the Trust’s sustained mentorship, they receive comprehensive support that transforms their academic journey and expands their aspirations. These efforts may appear small, but their impact is deeply transformative.

The Sanskar and Personality Development Sessions help students understand the true purpose of learning. They begin studying for knowledge instead of marks, and their academic performance naturally improves. Alongside education, the girls absorb the core values of Indian culture—humility, cooperation, mutual respect, environmental awareness, and reverence for nature.

To nurture core values, girls are inspired to embrace 3 sacred resolutions — simple yet transformative steps toward inner and outer growth:

🙏
Faith

Offer daily prayers with selfless devotion and gratitude for at least 10 minutes.

🎯
Focus

Pursue academic goals with dedication, discipline, and a strong sense of purpose.

❤️
Fulfillment

Support other needy sisters once self-reliant, nurturing compassion and belonging.

Together, these initiatives highlight why Sanskar is equally essential as academic support. It shapes not just educated individuals, but confident, compassionate, and responsible human beings.

How These Classes Help in Holistic Development

Academic and Personal Growth

The Trust empowers girls academically and nurtures their holistic personal growth. Mentorship fosters self-confidence, positive thinking, mental resilience, and a genuine love for learning. These sessions help girls internalize values such as humility, compassion, tolerance, gratitude, and respect for others and the environment.

Empowerment and Professional Development

Girls develop self-reliance, dignity, and independence, pursuing professional degrees and skills training. Support from the Trust enables self-employment, wage work, and leadership in society.

Lifestyle and Value Transformation
Opposing dowry and child marriage: Girls boldly stand against social evils, advocating equality and justice.
Embracing Yoga, Pranayama, and meditation: Promotes holistic health and mental balance.
Encouraging parents to support daughters’ education: Builds a family-wide culture of learning.
Valuing and conserving natural resources: Water, air, sunlight—once taken for granted, now valued. These girls embrace sustainable living, nurture the environment, and inspire care for generations to come.
Improving hygiene, health, and childcare: Leads to healthier lives and lower infant mortality.
Valuing dignity of labour and earning independently: Instills self-respect and work ethics.
Inspiring intergenerational learning: Mothers attend sessions with daughters, fostering family harmony.
Positive Impact on Families and Society

Many girls have become compassionate mothers, responsible wives, and caring daughters-in-law, instilling strong values in their families. Homes once tense now radiate joy, harmony, and mutual respect, shaping responsible and compassionate citizens.

Through the combination of education, mentorship, and Sanskar-based guidance, these classes develop well-rounded, confident, and socially responsible individuals.
