Why Sanskar Matters

While modern education strengthens intellect, it often lacks the moral grounding needed to overcome ego, anger, greed, and jealousy.

Every human being is blessed by God with Buddhi (intellect) and Vivek (discretion). When refined through education, values, and spirituality, individuals develop humility, compassion, sincerity, generosity, and a positive outlook on life.

Through motivational and value-based guidance, girls develop environmental consciousness, a spirit of service, and reverence for nature. This thoughtful blend of education and Sanskar empowers them to grow into confident, balanced, and responsible individuals.

About The Classes

Conducted every Sunday at Manidweep, Jodhpur, these sessions go beyond academics.

Volunteers, mentors, and guest speakers guide students in personality, confidence, and life skills.

The Trust bridges the gap by offering continuous mentorship, counselling, and career guidance.
